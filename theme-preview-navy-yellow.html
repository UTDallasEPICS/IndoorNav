Navy & yellow

Six ways to use navy and yellow, which sit almost opposite each other on the color wheel. Yellow is too light to read as a thin line on the light map, so the route stays navy everywhere; yellow goes on the button, active floor, destination room or the whole sheet.

Solid: Find Route button, active floor, your arrow, set-location pin Accent: route line, destination pin and room, links, input underline
